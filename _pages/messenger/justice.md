Justice is tested by conflict, unequal power, and disagreement. These entries collect teachings and incidents concerning judgment, religious difference, treaties, captives, and the limits of fighting.

## Justice, judgment, and freedom from favoritism
{: #justice-judgment-and-freedom-from-favoritism }

- <span id="src-0294"></span>The Qur’an commands justice even toward people one dislikes and forbids hatred from distorting judgment.

    > "O believers! Stand firm for Allah and bear true testimony. Do not let the hatred of a people lead you to injustice. Be just! That is closer to righteousness. And be mindful of Allah. Surely Allah is All-Aware of what you do." (**Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:8**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ كُونُوا۟ قَوَّٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلْقِسْطِ ۖ وَلَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَـَٔانُ قَوْمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعْدِلُوا۟ ۚ ٱعْدِلُوا۟ هُوَ أَقْرَبُ لِلتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرٌۢ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ ۝٨﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ المَائـِدَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0301"></span>The Qur’an requires people to uphold justice even when the truth is against themselves, their parents, or close relatives.

    > "O believers! Stand firm for justice as witnesses for Allah even if it is against yourselves, your parents, or close relatives. Be they rich or poor, Allah is best to ensure their interests. So do not let your desires cause you to deviate ˹from justice˺. If you distort the testimony or refuse to give it, then ˹know that˺ Allah is certainly All-Aware of what you do." (**Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:135**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿۞ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ كُونُوا۟ قَوَّٰمِينَ بِٱلْقِسْطِ شُهَدَآءَ لِلَّهِ وَلَوْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَوِ ٱلْوَٰلِدَيْنِ وَٱلْأَقْرَبِينَ ۚ إِن يَكُنْ غَنِيًّا أَوْ فَقِيرًا فَٱللَّهُ أَوْلَىٰ بِهِمَا ۖ فَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلْهَوَىٰٓ أَن تَعْدِلُوا۟ ۚ وَإِن تَلْوُۥٓا۟ أَوْ تُعْرِضُوا۟ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ خَبِيرًا ۝١٣٥﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0308"></span>When people sought special leniency for a woman from an influential family in a theft case, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ rejected the request and said that even his own daughter would not be exempt from the law.

    > "By God, if Fatima daughter of Muhammad stole, I would apply the law to her." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 6788[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0313"></span>The Qur’an commands justice, excellence, and generosity while forbidding indecency, wrongdoing, and aggression.

    > "Indeed, Allah commands justice, grace, as well as courtesy to close relatives. He forbids indecency, wickedness, and aggression. He instructs you so perhaps you will be mindful." (**Sūrat al-Naḥl 16:90**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿۞ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَأْمُرُ بِٱلْعَدْلِ وَٱلْإِحْسَـٰنِ وَإِيتَآئِ ذِى ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ وَيَنْهَىٰ عَنِ ٱلْفَحْشَآءِ وَٱلْمُنكَرِ وَٱلْبَغْىِ ۚ يَعِظُكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ ۝٩٠﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

## Religious difference and coexistence
{: #religious-difference-and-coexistence }

- <span id="src-0322"></span>The Qur’an states that there is no compulsion in religion.[57](/messenger/sources#ref-57){: .footnote}[60](/messenger/sources#ref-60){: .footnote}

    > "Let there be no compulsion in religion, for the truth stands out clearly from falsehood. So whoever renounces false gods and believes in Allah has certainly grasped the firmest, unfailing hand-hold. And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing." (**Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:256**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿لَآ إِكْرَاهَ فِى ٱلدِّينِ ۖ قَد تَّبَيَّنَ ٱلرُّشْدُ مِنَ ٱلْغَىِّ ۚ فَمَن يَكْفُرْ بِٱلطَّـٰغُوتِ وَيُؤْمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱسْتَمْسَكَ بِٱلْعُرْوَةِ ٱلْوُثْقَىٰ لَا ٱنفِصَامَ لَهَا ۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ ۝٢٥٦﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0329"></span>The Qur’an tells Muslims to treat non-Muslims who are not fighting them or driving them from their homes with kindness and justice.

    > "Allah does not forbid you from dealing kindly and fairly with those who have neither fought nor driven you out of your homes. Surely Allah loves those who are fair." (**Sūrat al-Mumtaḥanah 60:8**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿لَّا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَـٰتِلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَـٰرِكُمْ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ ۝٨﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ المُمۡتَحنَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0336"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ warned that killing a non-Muslim protected by covenant is a grave sin.[57](/messenger/sources#ref-57){: .footnote}[60](/messenger/sources#ref-60){: .footnote}

    > "Whoever kills a person under covenant will not smell the fragrance of Paradise." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 3166[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0341"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ stood for the funeral procession of a Jewish man and, when his religion was pointed out, replied by emphasizing that he was still a human soul.

    > He said, "Was it not a soul?" — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 1312[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0346"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ visited a Jewish boy who had served him when the boy became ill. He invited him to Islam during the visit, but religious difference did not prevent the personal act of care.

    > "A Jewish boy used to serve the Prophet ﷺ, and he became ill, so the Prophet ﷺ went to visit him." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 1356[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0351"></span>The Qur’an forbids Muslims from insulting what other people worship, in part because doing so can provoke retaliatory insult and deepen hostility.

    > "˹O believers!˺ Do not insult what they invoke besides Allah or they will insult Allah spitefully out of ignorance. This is how We have made each people's deeds appealing to them. Then to their Lord is their return, and He will inform them of what they used to do." (**Sūrat al-Anʿām 6:108**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿وَلَا تَسُبُّوا۟ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَسُبُّوا۟ ٱللَّهَ عَدْوًۢا بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ ۗ كَذَٰلِكَ زَيَّنَّا لِكُلِّ أُمَّةٍ عَمَلَهُمْ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِم مَّرْجِعُهُمْ فَيُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَعْمَلُونَ ۝١٠٨﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ الأَنۡعَامِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0358"></span>The Qur’an tells Muslims to argue with Jews and Christians in the best manner, except where people act wrongfully.

    > "Do not argue with the People of the Book unless gracefully, except with those of them who act wrongfully. And say, 'We believe in what has been revealed to us and what was revealed to you. Our God and your God is ˹only˺ One. And to Him we ˹fully˺ submit.'" (**Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt 29:46**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿۞ وَلَا تُجَـٰدِلُوٓا۟ أَهْلَ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ إِلَّا بِٱلَّتِى هِىَ أَحْسَنُ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا۟ مِنْهُمْ ۖ وَقُولُوٓا۟ ءَامَنَّا بِٱلَّذِىٓ أُنزِلَ إِلَيْنَا وَأُنزِلَ إِلَيْكُمْ وَإِلَـٰهُنَا وَإِلَـٰهُكُمْ وَٰحِدٌ وَنَحْنُ لَهُۥ مُسْلِمُونَ ۝٤٦﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ العَنكَبُوتِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0365"></span>The Qur’an tells believers to invite others through wisdom, good instruction, and the best manner of debate.

    > "Invite ˹all˺ to the Way of your Lord with wisdom and kind advice, and only debate with them in the best manner. Surely your Lord ˹alone˺ knows best who has strayed from His Way and who is ˹rightly˺ guided." (**Sūrat al-Naḥl 16:125**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿ٱدْعُ إِلَىٰ سَبِيلِ رَبِّكَ بِٱلْحِكْمَةِ وَٱلْمَوْعِظَةِ ٱلْحَسَنَةِ ۖ وَجَـٰدِلْهُم بِٱلَّتِى هِىَ أَحْسَنُ ۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ ۖ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِٱلْمُهْتَدِينَ ۝١٢٥﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0372"></span>In explaining why aggression may be resisted, the Qur’an names monasteries, churches, synagogues, and mosques among houses of worship whose destruction is to be resisted.

    > "˹They are˺ those who have been expelled from their homes for no reason other than proclaiming: 'Our Lord is Allah.' Had Allah not repelled ˹the aggression of˺ some people by means of others, destruction would have surely claimed monasteries, churches, synagogues, and mosques in which Allah's Name is often mentioned. Allah will certainly help those who stand up for Him. Allah is truly All-Powerful, Almighty." (**Sūrat al-Ḥajj 22:40**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿ٱلَّذِينَ أُخْرِجُوا۟ مِن دِيَـٰرِهِم بِغَيْرِ حَقٍّ إِلَّآ أَن يَقُولُوا۟ رَبُّنَا ٱللَّهُ ۗ وَلَوْلَا دَفْعُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلنَّاسَ بَعْضَهُم بِبَعْضٍ لَّهُدِّمَتْ صَوَٰمِعُ وَبِيَعٌ وَصَلَوَٰتٌ وَمَسَـٰجِدُ يُذْكَرُ فِيهَا ٱسْمُ ٱللَّهِ كَثِيرًا ۗ وَلَيَنصُرَنَّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَنصُرُهُۥٓ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَقَوِىٌّ عَزِيزٌ ۝٤٠﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ الحَجِّ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0379"></span>When Asma bint Abu Bakr asked whether she should maintain relations with her polytheist mother, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told her to continue treating her mother well.

    > He said, "Yes. Maintain good relations with your mother." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 2620[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0384"></span>Before sending Muadh to Yemen, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ warned him not to oppress people because the prayer of an oppressed person has no barrier before God.

    > "Beware the supplication of the oppressed, for there is no barrier between it and God." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 2448[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0389"></span>In a hadith reporting words from God, oppression is forbidden among people without limiting the rule to a particular tribe, religion, or dispute.

    > "My servants, I have forbidden oppression for Myself and made it forbidden among you, so do not oppress one another." — *Sahih Muslim* 2577a[3](/messenger/sources#ref-3){: .footnote}[14](/messenger/sources#ref-14){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0394"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ acknowledged that one litigant might argue more persuasively than another and warned that a favorable court ruling could not make someone else’s property morally lawful to take.

    > "I am only human... perhaps one of you is more eloquent in presenting his case. If I award him his brother's right, let him not take it." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 7169[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0399"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught that a judge should not decide a case while angry.

    > "No judge should judge between two people while he is angry." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 7158[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0404"></span>The Qur’an requires Muslims to grant protection to an enemy polytheist who asks for it and then escort that person to safety.

    > "And if anyone from the polytheists asks for your protection ˹O Prophet˺, grant it to them so they may hear the Word of Allah, then escort them to a place of safety, for they are a people who have no knowledge." (**Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:6**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿وَإِنْ أَحَدٌ مِّنَ ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ ٱسْتَجَارَكَ فَأَجِرْهُ حَتَّىٰ يَسْمَعَ كَلَـٰمَ ٱللَّهِ ثُمَّ أَبْلِغْهُ مَأْمَنَهُۥ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ قَوْمٌ لَّا يَعْلَمُونَ ۝٦﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0411"></span>When Jewish visitors greeted Prophet Muhammad ﷺ with a hostile pun and Aisha responded angrily, he restrained her and told her that God loves gentleness.

    > "Be calm, Aisha. God loves gentleness in all matters." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 6024[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0416"></span>The Qur’an teaches individual moral responsibility: no person bears another person’s sin merely because of family, tribe, religion, or group identity.

    > "Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ 'Should I seek a lord other than Allah while He is the Lord of everything?' No one will reap except what they sow. No soul burdened with sin will bear the burden of another. Then to your Lord is your return, and He will inform you of your differences." (**Sūrat al-Anʿām 6:164**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿قُلْ أَغَيْرَ ٱللَّهِ أَبْغِى رَبًّا وَهُوَ رَبُّ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ ۚ وَلَا تَكْسِبُ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ إِلَّا عَلَيْهَا ۚ وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٌ وِزْرَ أُخْرَىٰ ۚ ثُمَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكُم مَّرْجِعُكُمْ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمْ فِيهِ تَخْتَلِفُونَ ۝١٦٤﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ الأَنۡعَامِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0423"></span>The Qur’an repeatedly tells Prophet Muhammad ﷺ that his duty is to convey the message, not to force people to believe.

    > "Had your Lord so willed ˹O Prophet˺, all ˹people˺ on earth would have certainly believed, every single one of them! Would you then force people to become believers?" (**Sūrat Yūnus 10:99**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿وَلَوْ شَآءَ رَبُّكَ لَـَٔامَنَ مَن فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ كُلُّهُمْ جَمِيعًا ۚ أَفَأَنتَ تُكْرِهُ ٱلنَّاسَ حَتَّىٰ يَكُونُوا۟ مُؤْمِنِينَ ۝٩٩﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ يُونُسَ</span></div>

## War, noncombatants, captives, and the limits of fighting
{: #war-noncombatants-captives-and-the-limits-of-fighting }

- <span id="src-0432"></span>The Qur’an permits fighting those who wage war while explicitly forbidding transgression.[58](/messenger/sources#ref-58){: .footnote}[59](/messenger/sources#ref-59){: .footnote}

    > "Fight in the cause of Allah ˹only˺ against those who wage war against you, but do not exceed the limits. Allah does not like transgressors." (**Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:190**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿وَقَـٰتِلُوا۟ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقَـٰتِلُونَكُمْ وَلَا تَعْتَدُوٓا۟ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُعْتَدِينَ ۝١٩٠﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0439"></span>After a woman was found dead during a campaign, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ expressly forbade intentionally killing women and children in war.[58](/messenger/sources#ref-58){: .footnote}[59](/messenger/sources#ref-59){: .footnote}

    > "The Messenger of God ﷺ forbade the killing of women and children." — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 3015[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0444"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ instructed military commanders not to embezzle, betray agreements, mutilate bodies, or kill children.

    > "Do not embezzle, do not act treacherously, do not mutilate, and do not kill a child." — *Sahih Muslim* 1731a[3](/messenger/sources#ref-3){: .footnote}[14](/messenger/sources#ref-14){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0449"></span>The Qur’an tells Muslims to accept peace when the enemy genuinely inclines toward peace.

    > "If the enemy is inclined towards peace, make peace with them. And put your trust in Allah. Indeed, He ˹alone˺ is the All-Hearing, All-Knowing." (**Sūrat al-Anfāl 8:61**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}

    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0456"></span>The Qur’an explicitly praises feeding captives, placing them beside the poor and orphans as people whose needs should be met.

    > "and give food—despite their desire for it—to the poor, the orphan, and the captive." (**Sūrat al-Insān 76:8**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}

    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0463"></span>The Qur’an permits proportionate retaliation but repeatedly presents patience and forgiveness as the better response where possible.

    > "And whoever is patient and forgives - indeed, that is of the matters [requiring] determination." ## Treaties, protection, judgment, and obligations across conflict
{: #treaties-protection-judgment-and-obligations-across-conflict }

- <span id="src-0522"></span>The Qur’an requires treaties with religious opponents to be honored for as long as the other party continues to honor them.

    > "As for the polytheists who have honoured every term of their treaty with you and have not supported an enemy against you, honour your treaty with them until the end of its term. Surely Allah loves those who are mindful ˹of Him˺." (**Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:4**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ عَـٰهَدتُّم مِّنَ ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَنقُصُوكُمْ شَيْـًٔا وَلَمْ يُظَـٰهِرُوا۟ عَلَيْكُمْ أَحَدًا فَأَتِمُّوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ عَهْدَهُمْ إِلَىٰ مُدَّتِهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُتَّقِينَ ۝٤﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ التَّوۡبَةِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0529"></span>The Qur’an treats keeping covenants as a religious duty before God, not merely a matter of diplomatic convenience.

    > "Honour Allah's covenant when you make a pledge, and do not break your oaths after confirming them, having made Allah your guarantor. Surely Allah knows all you do." (**Sūrat al-Naḥl 16:91**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿وَأَوْفُوا۟ بِعَهْدِ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا عَـٰهَدتُّمْ وَلَا تَنقُضُوا۟ ٱلْأَيْمَـٰنَ بَعْدَ تَوْكِيدِهَا وَقَدْ جَعَلْتُمُ ٱللَّهَ عَلَيْكُمْ كَفِيلًا ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَفْعَلُونَ ۝٩١﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ النَّحۡلِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0536"></span>In the traditional account behind Qur’an 4:105–112, members of a Muslim clan tried to blame a Jewish man for stolen property, and the revealed passage warned Prophet Muhammad ﷺ not to defend the deceitful.

    > "Indeed, We have sent down the Book to you ˹O Prophet˺ in truth to judge between people by means of what Allah has shown you. So do not be an advocate for the deceitful." (**Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:105**), Dr. Mustafa Khattab, *The Clear Quran*[1](/messenger/sources#ref-1){: .footnote}[11](/messenger/sources#ref-11){: .footnote}[12](/messenger/sources#ref-12){: .footnote}[36](/messenger/sources#ref-36){: .footnote}; occasion report: *Jami at-Tirmidhi* 3036, graded *hasan* [good][5](/messenger/sources#ref-5){: .footnote}[16](/messenger/sources#ref-16){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    >
    > <div dir="rtl" lang="ar" class="quran-arabic">﴿إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَآ إِلَيْكَ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ بِٱلْحَقِّ لِتَحْكُمَ بَيْنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِمَآ أَرَىٰكَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ وَلَا تَكُن لِّلْخَآئِنِينَ خَصِيمًا ۝١٠٥﴾ <span class="ayah-ref">سُورَةُ النِّسَاءِ</span></div>
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0543"></span>An irrigation dispute involving Prophet Muhammad’s ﷺ cousin al-Zubayr preserved even the other litigant’s accusation that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ had favored a relative. The report, including the accusation, was transmitted by al-Zubayr’s own son.

    > Muhammad ﷺ first told al-Zubayr to water and then release the water to his neighbor; after the objection, he ordered him to take his full right. — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 2359-2362[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0548"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught that a captive mother should not be separated from her child.

    > "Whoever separates a mother from her child, God will separate him from those he loves on the Day of Resurrection." — *Jami at-Tirmidhi* 1566, graded *hasan* [good][5](/messenger/sources#ref-5){: .footnote}[16](/messenger/sources#ref-16){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

## Judicial caution and limits on authority
{: #judicial-caution-and-limits-on-authority }

- <span id="src-0909"></span>When Ma’iz repeatedly confessed to adultery, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ turned him away several times and investigated whether he understood what he was saying and whether the legal conditions applied before accepting the confession.

    > The report describes Ma’iz returning repeatedly before the confession was finally accepted. — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 6825[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }

- <span id="src-0914"></span>Prophet Muhammad ﷺ openly acknowledged that a court can reach the wrong result if one litigant presents a more persuasive case, and he warned that a favorable ruling does not make another person’s property morally lawful.

    > “I am only a human being. You bring me your disputes, and one of you may be more persuasive than another... If I award him something from his brother’s right, let him not take it.” — *Sahih al-Bukhari* 2680[2](/messenger/sources#ref-2){: .footnote}[13](/messenger/sources#ref-13){: .footnote}[43](/messenger/sources#ref-43){: .footnote}
    {: .quote }
