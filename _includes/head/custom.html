A humanist sans that complements Source Serif 4 without competing with either the body serif or the Abril Fatface title. Self-hosted under assets/fonts/ rather than loaded from fonts.googleapis.com/fonts.gstatic.com (audit P2-2): that path cost a render-blocking stylesheet fetch from a third-party origin, then a second round trip to a *different* origin for the files themselves, with nothing preloadable and `display=swap` guaranteeing a FOUT on every cold load -- and it sent the reader's IP to Google on every page view for no benefit this site needs.
